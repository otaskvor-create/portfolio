What I did at Proconom

I started as a junior designer — wireframes, visual assets, client coordination. Over time I took on bigger responsibilities: leading the design phase on larger projects, introducing research practices, and building internal design workflows the agency did not have before.

UX Design & Research

User journeys, wireframes, user interviews, basic usability testing. I introduced the first UX review process — every launch was reviewed structurally.

UI Design

High-fidelity prototypes and final visual assets for developers. A consistent visual style across projects for the same client. I led the agency transition from Photoshop → Sketch → Figma.

Component Library

I built the agency's first shared component library. The result was a 30% shorter UI phase for new projects thanks to reusable patterns.

Client collaboration

Direct client contact — requirements workshops, design presentations, iteration on feedback. I learned to quickly distinguish what clients said from what they really needed.
