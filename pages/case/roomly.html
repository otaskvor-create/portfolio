Roomly — booking software website

Roomly is meeting-room booking software that, at the time, had no way to introduce itself to anyone. I designed and coded the website that gave the still-unreleased product its first public face.

Problem

The Roomly app had never been launched to the public. Before diving into design, we needed to figure out what content to include on the site, in what order, and define a visual style that the product would eventually inherit.

On top of that, everything needed clear copy that immediately explained what visitors were looking at — a challenge made harder by the fact that the unreleased product had no tangible visuals or social proof to rely on yet.

Design process

Sketches → wireframes → UI design → development. I moved quickly through first stage on purpose, so more time was left for the wireframes, where the real decisions got made.

Sketches

The first round of sketching produced several versions. I narrowed them down to the strongest ones and, rather than making any further decisions, moved on to the wireframing stage.

Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes in Figma, kept deliberately simple so switching direction wouldn't cost much time — sufficient to move straight on to high-fidelity screens.

Wireframe vs final design

UI design

future vision of the product itself shaped the final visual identity, with consistency across every element as the main priority for a user-friendly result.

Development

I built the entire site solo, aiming for efficient, error-free code and deliberately avoiding third-party libraries wherever possible — both to keep it copyright-clean and to keep it fast.

UI development

Result

A professional marketing website that satisfied both me as designers and the people I built it for — and along the way, a new visual identity took shape that would go on to influence the look of the product itself.

  • A landing page presenting visitors most important information about the product itself
  • A pricing page with a monthly/yearly toggle and clear plan tiers
  • End-to-end execution: from first designs to independently developing a fully responsive website
Color palette

Outcome

After the final prototypes were approved, I moved into development and built the whole site myself.
