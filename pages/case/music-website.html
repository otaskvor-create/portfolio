Časovač — band website

Časovač is a four-piece band that needed more than a link in their Instagram bio — a site to introduce the group, showcase their music and upcoming shows, and sell merch directly to fans. I led the project end to end, from the first conversations with the band to a self-manageable WordPress site they now run on their own.

Problem

The main challenge was the sheer scope of the project — owning the whole process alone, from needs analysis and conversations with the band, through information architecture and visual style, to implementation and launch.

On top of that came a more human problem: four band members with four slightly different visions had to be reconciled into a single visual direction — one that would feel authentic, stay consistent, and still make the band instantly recognisable.

Design process

The project ran iteratively, in close collaboration with the band. Regular check-ins let us validate direction early, align expectations, and avoid larger changes later in development.

Analysis & preparation

Started with joint consultations to understand each member's expectations and ideas. Together we defined the site's purpose, target audience, core content and required features — first a minimal version, then extended with everything the whole band agreed on.

UI/UX design

Structure and first wireframes, then a progression from low-detail concepts to high-fidelity screens — with a constant focus on information hierarchy, clear navigation and visual consistency. I also checked throughout that every element could realistically be built in WordPress.

Wireframe vs final design

Development

Implementation in WordPress with Elementor, plugins for content management, and custom HTML/CSS for the front end so the result matched the approved design as closely as possible. Built section by section, testing visual accuracy and responsiveness along the way.

Wordpress development

Result

The result is a responsive, custom-built WordPress website that matches the band's identity, clearly presents the most important content, and can be managed by the band on their own — completed and launched on schedule.

  • A homepage introducing the band, its sound and identity
  • A music section showcasing releases and recordings
  • An upcoming-shows section keeping fans up to date on concerts
  • A full merch store, integrated directly into the site
  • A contact section so fans, venues and press can reach the band easily
  • Content the band can update themselves, thanks to WordPress and Elementor
